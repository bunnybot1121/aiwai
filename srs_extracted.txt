SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION
SaveFlow AI
Churn Rescue Desk — RocketRide Buildathon
Final MVP Specification
Source basis: RocketRide Buildathon guide supplied by the team. The guide defines the Churn Rescue Desk as a system that monitors product usage, billing activity, support tone and key-contact changes; learns from healthy/churned accounts and intervention outcomes; scores churn risk; selects response playbooks; performs permitted actions; and routes higher-threshold actions to humans. The guide also requires RocketRide to be load-bearing.
1. Introduction
1.1 Purpose
SaveFlow AI is a web-based retention platform used by a particular SaaS company to protect its recurring revenue. It analyzes that company's customer accounts, identifies customers likely to churn, explains the reasons, recommends a rescue strategy, performs safe actions, routes consequential actions to a human, and records outcomes for future recommendations.
1.2 Scope
The buildathon MVP will model one fictional SaaS company, such as NovaCloud, with a realistic customer dataset. The system is not required to integrate with a real enterprise CRM or billing provider for the MVP. The architecture will expose CSV/webhook interfaces so real integrations can be added later.
1.3 Core Loop
WATCH → PREDICT → EXPLAIN → DECIDE → ACT → LEARN
1.4 Target Users
2. Product Overview
2.1 Example Company
For the demo, SaveFlow will operate for a fictional SaaS company named NovaCloud. NovaCloud has thousands of customers and wants to reduce preventable churn. The company supplies customer usage, billing, support and contact-change data to SaveFlow.
2.2 Example Scenario
A NovaCloud account shows declining product usage, unresolved support tickets, an overdue invoice and a key contact leaving the company. SaveFlow combines these signals, produces a high churn-risk score with evidence, retrieves similar historical cases, recommends an intervention, and requires human approval before a significant retention discount.
2.3 System Architecture
NovaCloud data → SaveFlow Web App → FastAPI backend → RocketRide SDK → RocketRide .pipe workflow → specialist agents → churn decision → memory/retrieval → playbook → validator → automatic action or human approval → outcome database → future recommendations.

4. RocketRide Requirements
4.1 Load-Bearing Role
RocketRide shall perform the core AI workflow. It must not be included only as a one-line SDK call or branding element. The .pipe files shall be version-controlled in GitHub.
4.2 Pipeline
Input/Webhook → validation
Parallel Usage, Billing, Support and Contact analysis
Churn Risk Agent
Historical memory/retrieval
Playbook Agent
Confidence and threshold validator
Automatic action or Human Gate
Outcome recording

4.4 SDK
The FastAPI backend shall use the RocketRide Python SDK to connect to the runtime, start/use the required pipeline, send account payloads, receive results and terminate executions correctly. The same pipeline should be usable locally and on RocketRide Cloud.
5. Data Requirements
6. User Interface
6.1 Dashboard
Show total customers, at-risk accounts, critical accounts, ARR at risk, pending approvals, intervention success and recent alerts.
6.2 Customer Page
Show account profile, ARR, churn score, confidence, evidence, usage/billing/support/contact health, historical interventions and current recommendation.
6.3 Human Review
Show recommendation, evidence, confidence, expected impact and historical context, with Approve, Reject and Escalate controls.
6.4 Analytics
Show risk distribution, ARR at risk, saved versus churned accounts, intervention success rate and playbook performance.
7. Business Rules
0–39 = Low risk.
40–69 = Medium risk.
70–84 = High risk.
85–100 = Critical risk.
Low-confidence decisions shall not be automatically executed.
High-impact financial actions shall require human approval.
Missing critical evidence shall reduce confidence and may require human review.
Every intervention shall have an outcome state.
Historical outcomes shall influence future playbook recommendations.

9. Error Handling
10. Security
Use .env for RocketRide/API credentials.
Commit .env.example but never real secrets.
Use synthetic/demo customer data for the public buildathon demo.
Record who approved or rejected a high-impact action.
Do not expose secrets in logs.
11. Testing & Acceptance
Valid CSV batch processes successfully.
Invalid records do not crash the full batch.
RocketRide returns structured risk, confidence and evidence.
High-risk/high-impact action stops at the human gate.
Approval executes/records the action.
Rejection prevents the action and records the decision.
Outcome is stored and retrievable later.
Historical outcome informs a later recommendation.
Dashboard shows batch count, runtime and cost.
All .pipe files are present in GitHub.
Project runs from a clean clone with .env.example.
12. MVP Scope
The MVP focuses on one fictional SaaS company, NovaCloud, and a realistic seeded dataset. It supports CSV batch ingestion, RocketRide AI analysis, churn scoring, explanations, historical memory, playbook recommendation, human approval, intervention tracking and analytics.
12.1 Out of MVP
Real Salesforce/HubSpot/Intercom/Stripe integrations.
Native mobile application.
Complex enterprise authentication.
Training a large custom ML model.
Dozens of dashboards or microservices.
12.2 Future Scope
Multi-tenant SaaS deployment.
CRM, billing and support integrations.
Real email/calendar automation.
Advanced predictive ML alongside agent reasoning.
Automatic playbook optimization from long-term outcomes.
13. Demo Flow
Open SaveFlow AI for NovaCloud.
Show customer base and ARR at risk.
Run a batch analysis.
Open a critical customer such as Acme Corp.
Show a high risk score and the evidence behind it.
Show RocketRide specialist-agent execution.
Show historical cases and recommended rescue playbook.
Trigger a high-impact discount recommendation.
Show Human Approval Required.
Approve the action.
Show intervention recorded and outcome/memory updated.
14. Final Acceptance Statement
SaveFlow AI is complete for the buildathon MVP when NovaCloud customer data can enter the web application, RocketRide performs the core multi-stage AI analysis, the system produces explainable churn risk, selects a rescue playbook, knows when a human must approve an action, records the result, and uses outcomes as future knowledge.
